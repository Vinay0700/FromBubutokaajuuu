Yoga + Walking Days

On rest days, 30-min yoga + a 5,000–8,000 step walk does wonders for your metabolism and mood.

🥗

Focus on Protein First

Hit your protein target before anything else. It preserves muscle while you lose fat, and keeps you full longer.

8 hours of sleep controls cortisol and hunger hormones. More sleep = faster fat loss. Non-negotiable.

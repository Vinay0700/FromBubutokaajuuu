Swetha's Corner

Your personalised wellness notes

🧘

Yoga on rest days

30-min yoga + 6,000 steps on rest days boosts metabolism and reduces cortisol far better than just sitting still.

🥗

Protein first, always

Hit your protein number every single day. It preserves muscle while losing fat and keeps you feeling full much longer.

🌙

Sleep is the cheat code

8 hours of sleep controls ghrelin and cortisol — the hormones that cause fat retention and hunger. Sleep more, lose more.

💆

Train with your cycle

Weeks 1–2 (follicular): energy is high — go heavier. Weeks 3–4 (luteal): lighter sessions and more walking is totally fine and smart.

✨

Skin + hair during cut

Add collagen powder to your protein shake. Omega-3s (fish oil or flaxseed) help skin stay elastic while you lose weight.
